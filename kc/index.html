Most change training explains the model and hopes people use it. This one ends with a real conversation people are ready to have on Monday.

Clients pursue change because they expect performance to improve. But when people are asked to work in new ways, results rarely rise immediately. Performance often dips first as people learn, adapt, and change behavior. The dip is where change gets expensive. KickstartChange builds the capability to moderate that dip by helping people avoid the missteps and delays that prolong it. Participants apply a proven framework to a current change challenge, then walk out with a clear message, a plan for the barriers in the way, and a real conversation already rehearsed.

A powerful frame to open the conversation

The Dip is a useful opening frame because clients recognize themselves in it immediately. It shifts the conversation from "Do you need change training?" to "Where is change getting expensive?". You cannot avoid the dip, but you can moderate it. That is the work KickstartChange does.

What participants walk out with
01
A compelling "why change"
A message built with the power of contrast. It names the threats of standing still and the opportunities of moving, so a real team can see the gap and act.
02
A WIIFM message and barrier plan
A "what's in it for me" message tailored to a specific stakeholder, grounded in their pains and gains, plus a plan for the barriers standing in the way of new behavior.
03
A practiced conversation
The Hallway Huddle: a real stakeholder conversation rehearsed and refined with peer feedback, plus a simple roadmap for the steps after the workshop.
How the workshop gets there
Ready Do they know?
Create clarity and urgency. Use contrast, the threats of inaction set against the opportunities of change, to craft a compelling "why change" before explaining the details.
→Produces a "why change" message
Willing Do they care?
Connect with what people actually care about. Use pains and gains to understand the roots of resistance and craft a message that resonates at a personal level.
→Produces a WIIFM message
Able Can they do it?
Shape the skills and conditions that make new behavior possible. Identify the More of and Less of behaviors, then bust the barriers in the way.
→Produces a barrier plan and practiced conversation
Not a long simulation. Not theory. Not generic awareness training. Same practical tools, same shared language, individual contributor to executive.
The premise underneath: change happens one person at a time. People struggle for one of three reasons. They don't know, they don't care, or they can't do what is being asked. The Ready, Willing, Able model teaches people to diagnose which one is in play, and respond the right way.

Proposal copy

Ready-to-paste language for written proposals and RFPs. Copy as-is or adapt to your client's voice.

Description
KickstartChange is a 120-minute hands-on change workshop that helps people at every level become ready, willing, and able to make change work. Participants bring a real change they are living through and work it through a proven framework, learning to create clarity with a compelling "why change," connect with what people care about, and shape the conditions that make new behavior possible. They leave with practical tools, a current change advanced, and an action plan to keep it moving. Over 90% of KickstartChange participants report improved change capabilities in just two hours.
Audience
Anyone navigating or supporting change in their work, whether they are a senior leader, a manager, or an individual contributor. Works well for intact teams, cross-functional groups, and open-enrollment cohorts across any industry. No prerequisites. Mixed-experience and mixed-level rooms work well, because each participant or pair brings their own real change into the room.
Learning and business outcomes
  • Built capability. Participants leave able to support change, not just react to it. The tools transfer to every change that follows.
  • Behavior change. Teams move from abstract agreement to observable action: clearer messages, fewer barriers, real conversations.
  • Faster adoption. A current change advances inside the workshop, with a roadmap for what comes next.
  • Reduced resistance. People learn to diagnose where resistance comes from and respond to it the right way.
  • A shared change language. One vocabulary across levels and functions, so change efforts stay coordinated.
  • Resilient, change-ready people. Capability that compounds as change keeps coming.
Format
120 minutes, easily extendable. 20 to 200 participants in-person, 20 to 50 virtually. Delivered live, virtual or in-person. Available in English, Spanish, and French. Cross-functional teams at any level, within any industry.

Who it's for

The ideal client, where to start discovery, and the fit signals to listen for.

Ideal client
Organizations with a real change underway, a project, a system implementation, a restructure, a new strategy, where the change will stall unless people are ready, willing, and able to act. The L&D, HR, or business-unit buyer wants something short and practical that applies to actual work, not another awareness session that does not transfer. Equally strong for clients building general change agility, where the goal is broader change readiness, not just support for one initiative.
Discovery questions to ask
  1. ResultsWhat are the desired results you are seeking to achieve, and how will you know it worked?
  2. BehaviorsWhat behaviors do people need to do more of, or less of, to achieve these desired results?
  3. ConditionsWhy are you not seeing more of those behaviors today? What is getting in the way?
  4. SkillsWhat new skills do people need to develop to support those new behaviors?
Not a fit if
  • The client needs deep, end-to-end change leadership capability (ExperienceChange is the better fit, or pair the two)
  • The buyer wants a lecture or a theory session rather than hands-on application

Programs
When leadership development needs real-work application
Situation

The client is building a leadership program for executives, managers, directors, emerging leaders, or high-potential talent, and wants participants to apply the learning to real work.

They are asking: How do we help leaders translate strategy into action? How do we build confidence leading through uncertainty and continuous change? How do we make the program more practical and applied?

Positioning

Change is where leadership becomes visible. A program can teach the model, but leaders grow by using it: helping people understand the why, working through resistance, moving from alignment to action. KickstartChange is the applied module where they practice that on a real business challenge, as an action-learning engine for senior leaders or a focused module for emerging ones.

Recommendation
KickstartChange for Leaders

Use it as a practical, applied module inside a broader leadership journey. It works especially well when the program needs a high-energy experience that fits a tight agenda and still produces useful work.

In practice

Multinational leadership program. KickstartChange ran as the action-learning engine for top executives across a 12-month, four-session program, advancing real business challenges rather than just teaching them.

Executive education, financial services. 200+ high-potential managers and directors, only two hours available, used to translate strategy into change leadership action.

Manufacturing, sales managers. After years of market volatility and double-digit price increases, sales managers used it to communicate change to customers with more clarity and confidence.

Roll-outs
When a common change language is needed across the org
Situation

Change is continuous, faster-moving, and increasingly part of everyone's role. Leaders and managers need to make clearer decisions, experiment to learn, and move work forward with more confidence and accountability.

They are asking: How do we build change capability beyond a small group of experts? Are managers equipped to lead through constant change?

Positioning

Senior leaders usually understand the strategy. The trouble is it does not always travel down to the managers who have to act on it. Left without a shared approach, they translate it unevenly: some communicate clearly while others rush to action, some address resistance head-on while others avoid it. The fix is role-based capability, deeper change leadership for senior leaders and practical, day-to-day change tools for managers and their teams.

Recommendation
ExperienceChange for leaders. KickstartChange for everyone.

Use ExperienceChange with senior leaders to build deeper capability in planning, leading, and implementing change at the organizational level. Use KickstartChange with managers and broader teams to build a practical shared foundation for day-to-day change leadership.

Global resource leader, 20,000 employees. ExperienceChange built deep capability with directors and above. KickstartChange scaled practical change methods to supervisors in English, Spanish, and French, delivered by internal high-potentials. One change language across every level.

Initiatives
A new strategic initiative means new behaviors across the org
Situation

The client is launching a new strategic initiative, transformation, system implementation, restructure, or new way of working.

They are asking: Are managers equipped to carry the change? How do we help teams understand what this means for the org and for them? How do we create ownership instead of just awareness?

Positioning

Managers sit in the middle of every initiative, expected to translate the strategy and answer the hard questions, usually without the tools to localize it. People need agency, not just direction. The goal is not to repeat the strategy, which is where most initiatives stall, but to help people make sense of it, see their role, and act, so the rollout embeds the strategy into the culture instead of cascading past it.

Recommendation
KickstartChange for leaders and managers

It helps managers localize the message, build commitment, address barriers, and support the behavior change a strategy needs to take root. Participants leave with a shared language, practical tools, and a clearer way to help people understand the change, see their role in it, and act differently after the rollout.

In practice

Pharma M&A. 200+ global leaders and middle managers from both sides of a merger used it to lead integration consistently, building common language and real progress on integration challenges.

Professional services, ERP implementation. Cross-functional leaders built shared language and tools to bring their teams through a major system change without losing momentum.

Initiatives · AI adoption
When AI technical training isn't enough
Situation

The client has rolled out AI tools, invested in licenses and training, and expects gains. Curiosity was high at first, but usage is uneven. Some people are experimenting, some avoiding it, and many are unsure how AI applies to their real work.

They are asking: Why aren't people using this more, or well? How do we turn the promise of AI into business impact?

Positioning

AI adoption is not a technology problem. It is a people problem. Most rollouts stop at tool access and basic training, but usage stalls when people are not ready, willing, and able to use AI in real work: unsure why it matters, wary of it, or short on the practice and permission to change how they work. The answer is not more technical training, it is mobilizing people around a new way of working.

Recommendation
KickstartChange for AI adoption

Run it as a short, applied workshop where leaders, managers, and teams work AI adoption as the real change challenge. Participants clarify the case for change, identify stakeholder pains and gains, diagnose where adoption is getting stuck, and leave with a practical gameplan for the days after the session.

It pairs especially well with WBWAI 201, which helps people find the right workflows to unlock value.

In practice

Healthcare AI implementation. 120 medical practitioners adopting a new AI-enabled workflow. KickstartChange shifted communication from command-and-control to empathy, engagement, and influence.

Executive education, public agency. AI adoption started with the leadership team, treated as a trust, messaging, and stakeholder challenge rather than a tools rollout.

Sales FAQ

Sharp answers to the questions partners hear most.

How is this different from ExperienceChange?
ExperienceChange is the 5 to 8 hour simulation for leaders and teams driving organization-wide change. It builds deep, end-to-end understanding of how to plan and lead a full change initiative. KickstartChange is the two-hour applied workout for everyone supporting change in day-to-day work. It is more tactical and immediately practical. Where ExperienceChange covers the what and when of a change plan, KickstartChange focuses on the how. Sell either alone, or pair them for a journey across both levels.
Is this just for leaders, or for everyone?
Both. Leaders are the majority of participants, and the workshop carries a clear leadership lens, surfacing the traps leaders fall into, such as rushing to the "what," relying on one-size-fits-all messaging, and treating change like a checkbox. But the Ready, Willing, Able model works for anyone in an organization. The practical tools make it easy for any participant to take part and make progress, regardless of their change experience.
What do participants leave with?
Three things they can use right away: a compelling "why change" message built with the power of contrast, a "what's in it for me" message tailored to a real stakeholder along with a plan for the barriers in the way, and a rehearsed stakeholder conversation captured in the Hallway Huddle tool. They also leave with a simple roadmap for the steps after the workshop and a shared change vocabulary their team can keep using.
How does the AI integration work, and what about data?
AI support is flexible to the client's governance. By default, participants can use Copy for AI with the tools their organization has already approved. Where the client allows it, they can use the in-platform Refine feature instead. Either way, participants work with their own change challenge and their own context. The workshop runs within the client's environment and their rules.
Can our facilitators deliver it?
Yes. KickstartChange is designed to be easy to facilitate, with a simplified intro and a lighter facilitation load, while keeping the experience grounded in participants' real work. Your facilitators can deliver it after a short certification, and the platform now does more of the heavy lifting on timing and team progress. Where you prefer, ExperiencePoint can provide facilitators instead.
What if the client needs deeper change capability than two hours can build?
KickstartChange is strongest when the need is short, practical, change-related, and tied to real work. When a client needs deep, end-to-end change leadership capability, that is where it pairs with ExperienceChange, and with Spark for sustainment, as part of a longer journey. Position KickstartChange as the applied module inside that journey rather than the whole answer.
