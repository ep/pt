01

What it is

A short, practical change workshop that turns real change into clear messages, fewer barriers, and practiced conversations.

KickstartChange is a 120-minute applied change workshop for teams who are living through change and need to make it work. Participants bring a real change challenge, work it through Ready, Willing, Able, and leave with a clear message, a barrier plan, and a practiced stakeholder conversation.

What participants walk out with
01
A compelling Why Change message
A clear case for change built with the power of contrast: the risks of standing still and the opportunities of moving forward.
02
A WIIFM message and barrier plan
A stakeholder-specific message grounded in pains and gains, plus a practical plan for the barriers that could slow adoption.
03
A practiced conversation
The Hallway Huddle: a real stakeholder conversation rehearsed and refined with peer feedback before participants leave the room.
Not theory. Not a long simulation. Not generic awareness training.
KickstartChange gives people a practical way to diagnose why change is stalling: people don’t know, don’t care, or can’t do what is being asked. Ready, Willing, Able helps them respond the right way.
02

When to use it

Use KickstartChange when the client needs a short, practical, applied experience tied to real work.

Listen for these buyer cues
Short and practical
“We only have a few hours.” “We need something short and impactful.” “It has to fit inside a full agenda.”
Applied to real work
“We need people applying this to real work.” “We need to demonstrate ROI.” “We have done awareness training. Nothing stuck.”
Important change underway
“This initiative is a really important change for us.” “We need people aligned around it.” “Managers need help communicating it.”
Broader change readiness
“Our culture needs greater agility and resilience.” “We expect everyone to be a leader of change.” “Change is part of everyone’s role.”
Fit check
Best fit
The client wants a short, energizing workshop that helps people work on a real change challenge and leave with something they can use immediately.
Not the best fit
The client needs deep, end-to-end change leadership capability. Recommend ExperienceChange instead, or pair ExperienceChange for leaders with KickstartChange for broader scale.

Proposal copy

Ready-to-paste language for written proposals and RFPs. Copy as-is or adapt to your client’s voice.

Description
KickstartChange is a 120-minute hands-on change workshop that helps people at every level become ready, willing, and able to make change work. Participants bring a real change they are living through and work it through a proven framework, learning to create clarity with a compelling Why Change message, connect with what people care about, and shape the conditions that make new behavior possible. They leave with practical tools, a current change advanced, and an action plan to keep it moving.
Learning and business outcomes
  • Built capability. Participants leave able to support change, not just react to it.
  • Behavior change. Teams move from abstract agreement to observable action: clearer messages, fewer barriers, real conversations.
  • Faster adoption. A current change advances inside the workshop, with a roadmap for what comes next.
  • A shared change language. One vocabulary across levels and functions, so change efforts stay coordinated.
Format
120 minutes, easily extendable. Delivered live, virtual or in-person. Works for intact teams, cross-functional groups, and open-enrollment cohorts. Available in English, Spanish, and French. No prerequisites.
