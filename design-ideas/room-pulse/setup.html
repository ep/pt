Serves 12 to 200

Ingredients

  • 1 question
  • How are you feeling about AI?
  • 8 words about AI
  • 2 pulses
  • a second feeling, if you like
  • a “what moved you?” to finish
  • sound, to taste

Method

  1. 1.Open the room as people arrive.
  2. 2.Look before you share.
  3. 3.Show the room.
  4. 4.Check in again halfway.
  5. 4.Check in again before they leave.
  6. 5.Watch the shift.
