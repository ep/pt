The AI Impact Ladder

Four questions leaders ask about AI, in the order value usually builds.

Rungs can move at the same time. The order shows where to look when something stalls.

Why these four
  • Each rung asks a different question, has a different owner, and moves on a different clock: weeks for use, months for changed work, a year or more for results.
  • Using and changing are split on purpose. Nearly three-quarters of the organizations getting the most from AI have fundamentally redesigned workflows (McKinsey, 2026). People in companies redesigning work are 24 points more likely to see measurable business improvement (BCG, 2026).
  • It lines up with what you may already use: Microsoft's Copilot Dashboard (readiness, adoption, impact), the Kirkpatrick model (learning, behavior, results), and Prosci's point that installed does not equal integrated.
